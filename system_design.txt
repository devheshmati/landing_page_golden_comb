5. MOTION & INTERACTION RULES (GSAP + LOTTIE + LENIS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Dual Layer Rendering: Layer 1 = Bee Character Lottie, Layer 2 = Blurred Shadow Lottie.
- Dynamic Shadow Scale: Shadow expands/shrinks inversely with bee virtual height.
- Smooth Scroll Sync : Lenis smooth scroll velocity drives GSAP animation progress cleanly.
